Too Lazy, Still Raiding - Support

(Korean title: 귀찮은데 인던은 가고 싶어) A healer game where you just run dungeons, with no leveling and no farming. Your three party members fight on their own, and all you do is heal and dodge the floor hazards.

Contact

FAQ

My purchased items are gone / I switched devices.
Sign in with the same store account (App Store or Google Play), then tap the Restore Purchases button below the outfit shop in the game.

I bought Remove Ads but I still see ads.
Tap Restore Purchases once. If that doesn't fix it, please email us.

How do I get a refund?
On iOS, refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com); on Android, by Google Play. Refunds cannot be issued inside the app.

Where is my game data stored?
Only on your device. If you delete the app, your data is deleted too.
