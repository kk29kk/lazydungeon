Privacy Policy

App name: Too Lazy, Still Raiding (Korean title: 귀찮은데 인던은 가고 싶어; package com.samyeonsang.lazydungeon)
Developer: KIM DO HYUN (individual developer, Samyeonsang)
Effective date: October 20, 2026 · Last updated: October 28, 2026 (online leaderboard section added)

1. Information we collect

This app has no sign-up and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Your game data (scores, unlocked skills, outfits you are wearing, and settings) is stored on your device. For the online leaderboard (section 4 below), the app sends only your total score and a random anonymous ID that is generated once per device to the server. This ID is not linked to your name, email address, advertising ID, or phone number.

2. Ads

This app shows Google AdMob ads (a banner on the start screen and rewarded ads that you watch only if you choose to). To serve and measure ads, AdMob may use your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID, or IDFA on iOS), approximate location (based on IP address), and device information. On iOS, the app asks whether to allow tracking the first time you run it; if you decline, ads are shown without the advertising ID. In Europe and other regions where consent is required, Google's consent dialog appears first. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and the AdMob help page. You can turn off ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

3. In-app purchases

Paid items (Remove Ads and cosmetic items such as weapon element effects) are purchased through Google Play Billing on Android and the Apple App Store on iOS. Payment information is handled by Google Play or Apple, and the app only checks whether an item was purchased. The app has no access to payment information such as card numbers. With the same store account, you can bring your purchases back on another device using "Restore Purchases".

4. Online leaderboard

To show the online ranking in the "Records" screen (your rank, the total number of players, and your top percentage), the app sends your total score (the sum of your best scores for each boss) and your anonymous ID to Talo, an open-source game backend whose servers are in Germany, where they are stored. The app does not ask for a nickname, and other players see only the rank and score statistics. If there is no internet connection, nothing is sent and the app tries again later. For how Talo handles personal data, see the Talo Privacy Policy.

5. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Deleting your data

When you uninstall the app, the game data and anonymous ID stored on your device are deleted with it. The leaderboard entry that remains on the server (anonymous ID and total score) cannot be traced to any particular device, but if you email us your anonymous ID (shown in the Records screen), we will delete it.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date.
